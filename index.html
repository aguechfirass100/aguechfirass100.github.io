Cockpit AI — Context-Aware AI Assistant for MediaCockpit

Bertsch Innovation GmbH • Graduation Internship 2025–2026 • Enterprise PIM/DAM Platform
  • Shipped assistant inside an enterprise platform: built the conversational assistant directly into MediaCockpit (Apache Wicket portal + SSE streaming), then migrated it and the surrounding AI module from MC14 to the live MC15 release, where it runs as a production feature.
  • Bounded orchestration over a real capability surface: LangGraph4j 1.8.16 StateGraph with sequential and parallel-safe tool branches, hard turn guards (12 reasoning / 3 empty-semantic) and 23 annotated portal operations — every one of them application code, never raw model output against the data layer.
  • Retrieval improved by controlled experiment: dual-collection Qdrant retrieval with distribution-based score fusion raised NDCG@10 from 0.6986 → 0.7701, and a local BGE cross-encoder took it to 0.9067 (Recall@5 0.8405) on a 43-case semantic development set — with the CPU latency cost (103.3 ms → 26.3 s) measured and published rather than hidden.
  • End-to-end answers graded against real evidence: a 25-question Ragas suite scored 0.8338 mean faithfulness / 0.8539 answer relevancy over 23 eligible answers, after fixing the evaluator to capture the evidence the assistant actually received in the same request.
