Good context

What to include.

For a role

  • The team and what it owns.
  • The problem the role is meant to solve.
  • Location and remote expectations.
  • The interview process and timeline.

For a project

  • The task and the current failure mode.
  • What data or systems already exist.
  • The constraint that matters most: time, latency, privacy, or cost.
  • What a useful outcome would look like.
