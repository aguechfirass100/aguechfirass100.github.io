// 01. Background & Philosophy

Bridging Research & Production Reality

Building AI that doesn't just demo well, but survives enterprise workloads, ambiguity, and real-world edge cases.

I am an AI Engineer holding an Engineering Degree in Machine Learning & Data Science (2023–2026) and a Bachelor’s in Software Engineering (2020–2023). My work focuses on taking complex retrieval and multi-agent workflows from architecture and research into stable production environments.

At Bertsch Innovation GmbH (MediaCockpit), I designed and shipped an enterprise-scale context-aware assistant with a self-registering ecosystem of 14 LLM tools, dynamic LangGraph4j orchestration, and a dual-collection semantic retrieval pipeline utilizing Distribution-Based Score Fusion (DBSF).

I believe that what cannot be measured cannot be trusted. Whether fine-tuning embedding models with LoRA, engineering token-level ColBERT MaxSim rerankers, or deploying ReAct retrieval planners, every design choice I make is validated through rigorous offline IR metrics (nDCG@10, MRR, HitRate) before entering production.

Enterprise & Proprietary

Cockpit AI — Enterprise Context-Aware Assistant

Bertsch Innovation GmbH (MediaCockpit)
Java • LangGraph4j • Qdrant • Spring AI

Designed and engineered a production-grade multi-agent LLM ecosystem from the ground up to empower enterprise users to search, query, aggregate, and generate reports across large-scale digital asset catalogs.

  • Multi-Agent Graph Orchestration: Built with LangGraph4j featuring dynamic sequential and parallel tool-routing, looped self-correction, and persistent per-user conversation memory.
  • Autonomous Tool Ecosystem: Deployed 14 LLM-callable tools with a self-registering framework that auto-discovers and classifies tools by execution mode.
  • Intent Routing & Decomposition: Classifies natural-language inputs and routes to a ReAct retrieval agent or an LLM planner that decomposes ambiguous multi-part prompts into structured sub-queries.
  • Dual-Collection Retrieval: Node- and attribute-level embeddings merged via Distribution-Based Score Fusion (DBSF), significantly outperforming baseline RRF rank stability across 43 semantic test cases.

Presto — Arabic E-Commerce Hybrid Search & ColBERT Reranker

Leaderboard Finish Pipeline
View Repo

High-performance search and reranking architecture for complex, colloquial Arabic e-commerce product catalogs.

  • Embedding Fine-Tuning: Fine-tuned multilingual Qwen embeddings with LoRA and MultipleNegativesRankingLoss, evaluated on query-disjoint validation splits.
  • Custom ColBERT MaxSim: Built a token-level ColBERT reranker from scratch with chunked einsum operations, special-token masking, and pre-encoded catalog caching.
